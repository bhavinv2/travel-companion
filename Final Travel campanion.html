Everything you need to know before you travel

What is a Travel Companion?

A fellow traveller on the same route who keeps you company and helps with gates, forms, transit and baggage — in a language you're comfortable in.

How much does it cost?

Posting a trip, matching and connecting are free for the community.

Can I request one for my parents?

Yes — that's our most common request. You upload the ticket or fill the form; your parents don't need to do anything technical.

How does matching work?

We compare route, dates, flight, language and needs, then rank the closest matches with a score and reasons. A care‑team member reviews before anyone is introduced.

Is my information shared?

Only after both sides approve a match, and only for that match. We never sell or publish your details.

How long does matching take?

It depends on the route. Posting 30+ days before departure gives the best chance; we notify you as soon as a match appears.

Can I provide my flight details?

Yes. Upload the e‑ticket and we read the route, dates and flight number for you, or type them in manually.

Which countries do you serve?

India ↔ USA, Canada, UK, Australia and Germany are the most active routes. Other routes are welcome.
