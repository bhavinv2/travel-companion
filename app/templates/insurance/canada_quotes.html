    Good to know

    • Prices are estimates read live from Secure Travel’s published calculators (super visa, visitors) for RIMI’s Visitors to Canada plan. The final premium is confirmed from each traveller’s date of birth when the policy is issued.
    • Super visa cover is one full year (365 days) with at least $100,000 of emergency medical cover, as the super visa requires.
    • Each traveller is priced on their own; the totals add them up. A deductible is what you pay first on a claim: a bigger one lowers the price.
    • Monthly payment is the yearly price spread over 12 months, plus Secure Travel’s one-time monthly option fee.
    • Pre-existing conditions are covered when they have been stable for 90 days (under 70) or 180 days (70 to 84). Ages 85 to 89 with a pre-existing condition are not offered.
