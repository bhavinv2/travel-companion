Landing page

Controls for the public marketing landing (what signed-out visitors see at the home page). Colours restyle the landing instantly on save.

Contact e-mail

The landing "Request a call back" form is always saved to the CS console. Tick this to also e-mail it to the address below. This is the only e-mail this screen enables — nothing else.

WhatsApp numbers

Drives every Chat on WhatsApp button on the site — the landing page's floating button, the sign-up prompt, the Contact Us popup and the footer. With both numbers set, visitors pick the India or USA team (the likely one is pre-selected from their time zone). With one, the button opens it directly. With none, the buttons are hidden — nothing is shown until a real number is here.
