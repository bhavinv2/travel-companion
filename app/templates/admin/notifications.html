Notification switches

Stop notifications for everyone — completely, or only some channels / kinds. Users can additionally mute kinds they don't want at /settings/notifications; a notification goes out only when the global switch and the user's preference allow it. Sign-in and sign-up e-mails (e-mail verification, password reset, the invite for a new staff account) are not notifications and are never stopped here: switching them off locked people out of their own accounts.

What each kind covers

  • match alerts — the heads-up sent when a new matching trip appears (in-app + e-mail to the trip owner)
  • match intro — the contact-page link sent by "Notify both" / "Share my contact & notify" (e-mail or in-app). While off, those actions leave the side as pending without flagging CS.
  • connection — connection requests, accept / decline, and "trip modified / cancelled" for connected travellers
  • chat — "new message" bell notifications (chat itself keeps working)
  • announcements — blog-post notifications and admin broadcasts
  • cs — escalations, reported matches and "post claimed" alerts to CS agents
  • account — e-mail verification and password-reset e-mails (users cannot mute these; turning them off blocks password resets)
