Why does this travel companion service exist?

Because travelling far from home shouldn't mean travelling alone. Language barriers, unfamiliar airports and long layovers get easier with a trusted person beside you.

  • Language shouldn't be a barrierCommunicate in a language you're familiar with.
  • Travel details matterWe understand your itinerary before we match.
  • Human connectionA real person supports your journey.
