(?) How this works
  • Each line gives points (or takes them away) when a lead meets it. A lead’s score is the total, from 0 to 100.
  • “Ready to buy?” follows the score: Ready to buy from {{ score_state.hot }}, Thinking about it from {{ score_state.warm }}, else Not likely.
  • A rating somebody picked on the lead stays theirs: the score never changes it. Clear it on the lead to let the score decide.
  • Scores change when something happens on a lead, and again every night, because trips come nearer and replies get older.
  • “They replied”, “A quote was sent” and “A payment link was sent” count exactly what Sales results counts.
