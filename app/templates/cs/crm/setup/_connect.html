A new landing page

  1. Give the form a source code — any short name in lower case, e.g. diwali-insurance. Add the two lines below to the page. <form data-lead-source="diwali-insurance" data-product="TI"> <input name="name"> <input name="phone"> <input name="email"> <textarea name="message"></textarea> <button>Send</button> </form> <script src="{{ snippet_url }}" defer></script>
    Any other field in the form (dates, ages, cities) is saved with the lead. Ad clicks (utm, gclid, fbclid) are picked up by themselves.
  2. Nothing else is needed. The first lead from it appears under Where leads come from as needs set-up.
  3. Choose its pipeline once. Every lead after that goes straight in with its follow-ups planned.

A page on another website (or a developer) can send to {{ intake_url }} as JSON: {"source": "…", "name": "…", "phone": "+91…", "email": "…", "message": "…", "fields": {…}}. Other websites must be listed in CRM_INTAKE_ORIGINS.

Instagram & Facebook

{% if meta_ready %}

Connected Messages and comments on the Page and its Instagram account arrive as replies or new leads, and "Send" on a lead answers them there.

{% else %}

Not connected Until it is: on the lead page, open They wrote to us…, pick Instagram or Facebook and paste their message.

{% endif %} How to connect (once, by whoever manages the Facebook Page)
  1. In Meta for Developers, create an app (type Business) and add Messenger and Instagram. Link the Facebook Page to the Instagram account in the Page settings.
  2. Generate a Page access token for the Page, and copy the app's App secret. Choose any long random text as the verify token.
  3. Put them on the server as META_PAGE_TOKEN, META_APP_SECRET and META_VERIFY_TOKEN{% if meta_verify %} verify token set{% endif %}.
  4. In the app's webhook settings use {{ meta_url }} with the same verify token, and subscribe the Page to messages and feed, and Instagram to messages and comments.
  5. Meta reviews the app before it works for the public (permissions pages_messaging, instagram_manage_messages, pages_read_engagement, instagram_manage_comments). Until then it works for the Page's own admins, which is enough to test.
