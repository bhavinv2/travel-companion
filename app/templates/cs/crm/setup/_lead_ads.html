{# "Lead ads": how Facebook / Instagram and Google lead forms reach the CRM (Sales CRM 2.0, run 4b). #}

Lead ads

Leads from Facebook, Instagram and Google ad forms come in by themselves. Each form shows up above as its own source, needing set-up, until you choose its pipeline and who gets its leads. Until then nobody is messaged.

Facebook and Instagram forms {% if lead_ads.meta_on %}Connected{% else %}Not connected{% endif %}

  1. In your Meta app, ask for the permission leads_retrieval (and pages_manage_metadata to subscribe the Page), and use the same Page token as for Messenger and Instagram.
  2. In the Meta app's Webhooks, choose Page, use this address and subscribe to the field leadgen:
    {{ lead_ads.meta_url }}
  3. The first lead from a form creates the source “Facebook/Instagram form <number>”. Open it here and choose the pipeline.

Google Ads forms {% if lead_ads.google_on %}Ready{% else %}Not switched on{% endif %}

  1. Ask your developer to set a secret key on the server (CRM_GOOGLE_LEADS_KEY). Until it is set, this address answers “not found”.
  2. In Google Ads, on the lead form's Lead delivery step, paste this webhook URL and the same key:
    {{ lead_ads.google_url }}
  3. Use “Send test data” in Google Ads: test leads are accepted and not saved. Real ones become the source “Google Ads form <number>”.
