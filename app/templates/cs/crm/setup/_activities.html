(?) How this works
  • Each card is one way we find people. Press Edit to change it, then Save at the bottom.
  • Where it stands: "Ongoing" shows as running on How we sell. Green is running, amber is waiting on something, grey is paused or done.
  • For a campaign we pay for, fill the campaign part: the campaign key its links carry (utm_campaign), the lead source it feeds, its dates and what it cost.
