2. What we do

Connecting Desis helps travellers find companions on similar routes. We list travel requests, suggest matches, and — with your consent — pass contact details between matched travellers. We are an introduction service, not a travel agency, carer or escort service.

3. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you post and for verifying anyone you choose to travel with. Do not post false, misleading or harmful content, and do not share another person's details without their permission.

4. We do not vet companions

We do not run background checks. Meet in public places, verify identities and documents yourself, and use your own judgement. Connecting Desis is not responsible for what happens before, during or after your travel.

5. Contact details and consent

We share the contact details you give for a trip only with a matched companion and only with your consent. You may withdraw consent by closing your post or contacting us. Misusing contact details you receive (spam, harassment, resale) leads to immediate account termination.

6. Requests created by our team

If our team lists a request on your behalf from another platform, it is not published until you confirm it. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
