How it works

Your journey with NRI Parent Service

Post your trip, we find fellow travellers on your route, and you connect only when you're both happy — with a real care team watching over every match.

Do it yourself

Post your trip

A short guided form — or upload your e-ticket and we read the route, dates and flight for you. Tell us whether you need a companion, can offer help, or are open to either.

  • One-way, round trip or multi-stop
  • Flexible dates & nearby airports welcome
  • Post anonymously if you prefer
or
Our care team

We post it for you

Already asked around a Facebook or WhatsApp group? Our care team brings your request in and sends you a private claim link.

  • Confirm with one tap — no account needed first
  • Your contact details stay private until you claim it
1

Matches, scored and explained

The moment your post is live, we compare it with everyone heading the same way and rank the best companions — leg by leg, so someone can even join you for just part of a multi-stop journey. Every match comes with a score and the reasons behind it.

Same routeClose datesSame flightNeeds ↔ offers helpShared languagesYour preferences
2

See your matches — or find them yourself

Your matches land on your dashboard, each with a match percentage and why it fits. You can also browse everyone travelling your way in Desis on Move and reach out directly — posts that match one of your own trips are flagged for you.

Match % & reasonsBrowse all travellers“Matches your trip” flags
3

Connect, only when you both agree

Send or accept a connection request. Contact details are shared just for that match, and only after you say yes — until then, the private in-app chat keeps everything between you two.

You stay in controlNothing shared without a yesPrivate in-app chat
4

A real team stays close

People, not just algorithms. If a match goes quiet as your departure nears, our care team follows up personally, suggests alternatives and keeps you posted.

Follow-ups before take-offMatches who share your language
5

Fly together

Meet at the gate, share the long hours, help with bags, forms and transit — then land with a new friend and leave a review for the community.

Safe & friendlyLeave a review after
