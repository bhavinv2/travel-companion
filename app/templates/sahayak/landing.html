How it works

Four steps, and you can do all of them from abroad

  1. 1Tell us what is needed

    Pick the service, the address and whether it is urgent or for a particular day.

  2. 2We call to confirm

    Our team rings the number you give us, checks the details and agrees a time.

  3. 3A Sahayak visits

    A verified healthcare worker arrives, explains what they are doing, and carries out the service.

  4. 4You hear how it went

    The team follows up with what was done and what, if anything, needs attention next.
